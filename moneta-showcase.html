The project

Built to be useful, not overwhelming.

A personal finance workspace

MONETA started as a practical tool for tracking salary, expenses, monthly budgets and the money left over. The interface focuses on compact summaries, clear progress indicators and a straightforward monthly view.

Try it without an account

Explore a separate sandbox with fictional sample figures. Add and remove expenses, change the sample budget, switch months and export the demo data. Changes stay in this browser session and reset when you refresh.

  • No registration required
  • No connection to personal records
  • Designed for desktop and mobile
⌑
Your privacy comes first

This public demo uses fictional sample data only. It does not connect to the private MONETA account, its Supabase database or any personal financial records. The real application remains protected by its existing sign-in.
